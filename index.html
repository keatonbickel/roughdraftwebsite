AI Study App With Dr. Clarence "Skip" Ellis

How This Fixes A Problem

Our business wants to fix the problem of having no way to make a good time to study and how to study. That’s why we are creating “StudySync.” This app will give you set reminders of when, how long, and how to study and recommendations how to study for your upcoming test. This resolves the issue of students forgetting deadlines and upcoming tests and, in return, doing worse on their tests than they should be. This will boost school averages and could even be used to remind students of upcoming deadlines on assignments and reduce the amount of missing assignments.

Home Page Demo

This is what the home page would look like on the app. You can see the list of upcoming assignments and tests that this students has with buttons to show your schedule, assignments, an AI tutor, and a progression page to show how much you have grown. It even sorts the assignments and tests into each class and color codes each one for even better organization.
